# Wency Personal Website

## Project concept

This is Wency Miao's personal website.

The website represents two sides of the same person:

1. Academic
2. Elsewhere

The website should NOT feel like a conventional CV website.

## Entry experience

Visitors first arrive at a minimal landing page.

The page asks:

"How would you like to explore?"

Two options:

ACADEMIC
Research · Work · CV

ELSEWHERE
Life · Ideas · Creations

## Academic

The Academic section focuses on:

- Biology
- Public Health
- Epidemiology
- Data
- Research
- Professional experience
- CV

Visual language:

- restrained
- intellectual
- editorial
- elegant
- off-white
- deep navy
- serif + sans-serif typography

## Elsewhere

The Elsewhere section focuses on:

- Writing
- Books
- Music
- Travel
- Photography
- Personal projects
- Creative work

It can be more playful and experimental.

## Experience navigation

Do not present experience as a conventional CV list.

Each important experience should have its own custom icon.

Visitors click an icon to open the corresponding project/experience page.

Icons should use a consistent SVG visual system.

## Design principles

Avoid:
- generic startup aesthetics
- excessive gradients
- emoji as final icons
- clutter
- overly corporate design

Prefer:
- editorial design
- subtle animation
- whitespace
- custom SVG icons
- museum/archive influences
- restrained interactions
